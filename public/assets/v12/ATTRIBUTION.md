# Night track poster — v12

Locally rendered still of the actual adapted BMW M5 E60 and the browser track.
Front three-quarter view, working corona lights, AUTOSIM front and rear plates,
and additional photographic fill lights. No imported Assetto Corsa assets.

Car geometry: Whega, CC BY 4.0, source and adaptations in
../bmw-e60/ATTRIBUTION.md. Environment and surface sources are listed in
/credits.html and the adjacent track/environment provenance files. Horizontal
route derives from OpenStreetMap (ODbL); heights, finish furniture and lighting
are an authored reconstruction, not a laser scan. Club logo: АВТОСИМ.
